FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need my own Google Maps API key?

No. COAI Lead Engine handles all Google Maps data on our servers — you never touch an API key or a Google Cloud bill. Your scans just work.

What counts toward my quota?

Quotas are counted in API calls: each city search, results page, and contact-details lookup counts as one. A typical full scan (one category, ~40 businesses with phone + website enrichment) uses roughly 40 calls — so Starter's 1,500 calls cover about 30+ full scans a month, and Pro's 5,000 cover 100+. The free plan's 10 calls are enough to run a first real scan and see genuine results. Your live usage is always shown inside the app.

Is the data fresh?

Yes — every scan runs against live Google Maps data at the moment you search, not a stale exported database that's months old.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription yourself in one click from inside the app — no emails, no phone calls, no hoops.

Can I export my leads?

Always. Every plan (including Free) exports leads to CSV, so your data is yours.

Who is this for?

Agency owners, web designers, marketing consultants, and salespeople who sell to local businesses. If your customers are found on Google Maps, this finds them.

What if it's not for me?

Cancel anytime in one click — and every paid plan comes with a 7-day money-back guarantee. Email us at chaoticallyorganizedai@gmail.com within your first 7 days and we'll refund you in full, no questions asked.
